Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Email us at hello@joinlilah.com.

Does my parent need a smartphone?

No. Your parent can call Lilah from any phone, landlines included, or text from a basic cell phone. Family updates come by text, and you can choose who receives them.

Who answers when they call or text?

Lilah is an AI assistant that handles the conversation and the booking, backed by a real team that steps in whenever a person is the better choice.

Where is Lilah available?

We're opening early access area by area. Join the waitlist with your ZIP code and we'll let you know when Lilah is available near you.

Is Lilah a medical or emergency service?

No. Lilah helps with everyday, non-medical logistics like rides and errands. In an emergency, always call 911.

Can I sign up on my parent's behalf?

Yes. Most families set Lilah up together, and your parent stays in control of what's shared and with whom.

What will it cost?

We'll share pricing before launch. Everyone on the waitlist gets first access.
